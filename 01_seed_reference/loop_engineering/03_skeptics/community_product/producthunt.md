### 三、ProductHunt ProdShort 串（约 2026-06）怀疑面：maker 圈的循环失效词与 token ROI 质问

- URL：https://www.producthunt.com/p/prodshort/is-every-product-suddenly-becoming-an-ai-agent-3 （curl 实取 2026-10-06）
- 评论逐字：
  - Keesan："autonomous coding agents is the right direction of the future but we are far from that. anyone spending thousands a month in codex API knows that task drift, context bloat, blind retries, infinite looping, sub agent wastage, etc. is all real and it still needs human in the loop. We've experienced this first hand... eventually your CFOs gonna start asking for ROI on token spend. Firing engineers you pay $150K for and then spending $200K in tokens to do the same work in a way that is reliable, consistent, and PRD ready is the problem at an enterprise level."；"its not full agent until it can do autonomous work, self improve, self heal, consistent memory, and act like a managed worker, until then its a just a tool or assistant imo and i say this as someone who ships product and built 5 startups."
  - Zakaria empire："90% of agents fail at stage 2 because they don't have the right tools and the right planning logic behind them. In our tests, without a solid infrastructure, an agent's success rate is 23%. With the right layer of tools and error handling, we jump to 81%, and the cost drops by 60%."（数字为其单方主张，未核方法）
- 非 KOL 判断依据：PH 论坛普通 maker 回帖（Keesan 自述连创 5 家公司但无公开分发；Zakaria 为低互动账号）。
- **与 loop engineering 的挂钩**：**民间原生循环失效清单**——"task drift, blind retries, infinite looping, sub agent wastage"与推动档的目标漂移/无终止重试/预算失控三轴一一对应，由 maker 用自己的词说出；"not full agent until it can…self heal"另把 graph 档的自愈概念作了民间定义。反面钩：词原生出现但未接入 loop engineering 论述，其修复方案停在"right layer of tools"。
- **对原内容的强化/削弱**：强化（token ROI 质问与怀疑档既有成本线并读）；Zakaria 数据按单方主张降级使用。
