- 逐字摘录（全部实取）：

> "Tokenmaxxing - literally maximizing the amount of tokens you or your organization spends, tracked in leaderboards and rewarded with trinkets - was a mass delusion, something like a commercial form of AI psychosis."

> "Anthropic and OpenAI release a product, Agents, in the form of Claude Code/Cowork and Codex, respectively, that are basically lab employees working inside of customers' companies and are given company credit cards with no spending limit ... Employees who direct their Agents to use the most tokens are recognized as AI Innovators."

> "There was the consultant saying his client accidentally burned half a billion dollars on Claude Code. Amazon shut down its AI leaderboard. Legora CTO Jacob Lauritzen told Harry Stebbings that token leaderboards 'lead to tokenmaxxing, which is people just burn tokens just to look good. That's a really stupid way to do anything.' Ramp's Veeral Patel called it the Token Casino: 'useful software wrapped in mechanics that make spend feel like progress. It starts with the oldest trick in the book: abstract the money.' Palantir CEO Alex Karp told the TBPN boys that tokenmaxxing is like 'a porn addiction.'"

> "It took Uber, that last era's poster child of VC-subsidized demand, to break the spell. Its CTO said that the company had burned through its 2026 Claude Code token budget by April. In May, its COO said that the company was having a harder time justifying its AI spend, because the link between AI consumption and shipped features 'is not there yet.'"

> "Even Sam Altman, a prominent token vendor himself, admitted on CNBC that 'You hear companies saying, I am spending a ton of money on AI, and I know some great stuff is happening, but I know there's a ton of waste ... how long do I have to wait for it to really show up in revenue' It had become, he admitted, a 'huge issue.'"

> "we believe that there are more fundamental structural reasons for the negative ROT: Agents are the wrong architecture for most work."

> "The expert systems of the 1980s were exactly this dream ... They died of two diseases. Extraction was brutally expensive ... And the rules rotted, because when the world changed, updating them was manual and slow. ... AI collapses the cost of extraction, and the regenerate-and-retest loop cures the rot."

- **该条支持的最小主张**：tokenmaxxing→崩塌的完整行业叙事（Uber 4 个月烧穿年度预算／半亿美元误烧／Amazon 关排行榜／Altman 自认 "huge issue"）——"循环自主运行的成本面"在分析层被正式定价为 ROT（Return on Tokens）问题；同时给出反方架构论（agents 非最优架构）与解法（regenerate-and-retest loop）。
- 与 loop engineering 的挂钩：**预算与熔断**（正题名级：token 预算烧穿＝熔断实例链）＋**循环结构**（"Agents are the wrong architecture for most work" 的架构质疑＋regenerate-and-retest loop 作为替代）。
