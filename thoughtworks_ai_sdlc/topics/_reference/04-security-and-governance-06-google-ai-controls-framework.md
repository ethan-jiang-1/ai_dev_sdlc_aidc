## Why This Matters

This source is useful because it treats AI workloads as auditable systems with control drift, not just as prompts and models.

## Key Facts Captured

- Google says a typical generative AI workload is a complex ecosystem spanning AI platforms plus IAM, secrets, logging, storage, and networking.
- The article says it is essential not only to secure AI workloads, but to audit whether they adhere to compliance and whether controls drift over time.
- The framework uses NIST AI RMF and the Cyber Risk Institute profile as baselines.
- The goal is to move from manual checklist auditing to automated operational assurance.

## Research Use

- Supports Topic 04’s governance thesis that runtime audit and control monitoring must be part of the platform.
- Helps connect AI security to enterprise compliance operations instead of ad hoc reviews.

## Caveats

- Vendor-authored framework.
- Strong on audit structure, weaker on adversarial threat taxonomy.
