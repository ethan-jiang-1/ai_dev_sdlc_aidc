## Why This Matters

This source directly supports the seed hypothesis that the key bottleneck is no longer pure coding throughput. DORA frames AI as an amplifier of the underlying organizational system rather than an automatic productivity equalizer.

## Key Facts Captured

- DORA says AI’s primary role is to magnify an organization’s existing strengths and weaknesses.
- It also says the greatest returns on AI investment come from improving the underlying organizational system, not merely deploying tools.
- This implies that governance latency, team topology, and decision flow are first-order concerns in AI-native software delivery.

## Research Use

- Grounds Topic 02 in an organization-level framing instead of a tool-level framing.
- Supports the claim that middle-loop work exists because coordination, approval, and trust become the limiting system.

- This page is a report summary page, not the full PDF detail.
