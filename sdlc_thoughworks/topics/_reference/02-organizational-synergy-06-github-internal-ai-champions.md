## Why This Matters

This source provides a concrete operating model for scaling AI adoption inside large organizations. It is highly relevant to the seed’s “middle loop” concept because it describes people who coach, translate, and feed back reality from teams to leadership.

## Key Facts Captured

- GitHub says AI adoption is not just a technology problem; it is a change management problem.
- The company argues that internal advocates are the human bridge between strategy and execution.
- These advocates operate through peer-to-peer trust rather than executive authority.
- Their functions include making AI practical, helping peers clear early hurdles, and acting as a feedback loop.

## Research Use

- Supports the idea that a new coordination layer emerges around enablement, translation, and trust-building.
- Gives Topic 02 a concrete candidate pattern for institutionalizing middle-loop work without making it purely managerial.

## Caveats

- This is a GitHub playbook article, not an independent comparative study.
- It is prescriptive and experience-based rather than experimentally validated.
