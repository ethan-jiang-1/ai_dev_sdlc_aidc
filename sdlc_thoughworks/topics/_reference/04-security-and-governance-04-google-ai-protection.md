## Why This Matters

This source is useful because it frames AI security as lifecycle security, not just model filtering. That maps cleanly to Topic 04’s need for platform-level governance.

## Key Facts Captured

- Google positions AI Protection as risk mitigation across the AI lifecycle.
- It explicitly includes model and data protection across clouds and models.
- The article says AI Protection can use virtual red teaming to identify toxic combinations and attack paths.
- It emphasizes discovery of sensitive data in AI datasets and posture adjustment to reduce exposure.

## Research Use

- Supports Topic 04’s claim that AI governance must combine data visibility, runtime posture, and adversarial testing.
- Gives a practical example of “generated attacks to defend against generative attacks.”

## Caveats

- Product-oriented source from one vendor.
- Useful for control patterns, not a neutral comparative framework.
