## Why This Matters

This source is directly about autonomous agents rather than generic LLM apps. It is one of the most relevant current resources for Topic 04’s threat model.

## Key Facts Captured

- OWASP describes this as a globally peer-reviewed framework for the most critical risks facing autonomous and agentic AI systems.
- The framework is designed to give builders, defenders, and decision-makers a practical starting point for securing agents that plan, act, and decide across workflows.
- Related release materials describe risks such as `Agent Goal Hijack`, `Tool Misuse`, `Identity & Privilege Abuse`, `Agentic Supply Chain Vulnerabilities`, and `Unexpected Code Execution`.

## Research Use

- Supports Topic 04’s shift from “LLM security” to “agentic security.”
- Gives language for the behavior-layer risks created when agents act through tools and long-running workflows.

## Caveats

- Community-driven guidance, still relatively new compared with older security frameworks.
- Should be paired with mature identity and architecture frameworks such as NIST ZTA.
