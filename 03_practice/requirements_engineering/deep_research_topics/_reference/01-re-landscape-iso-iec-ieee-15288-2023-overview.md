## 关键事实

1. ISO 官方页确认：
   - `Reference number: ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023`
   - `Edition 2`
   - `Published 2023-05`
2. Abstract 明确写道：
   - 标准 `establishes a common framework of process descriptions`
   - 适用于 `systems of interest`, `system elements`, `systems of systems`
3. 它覆盖 full life cycle：
   - conception
   - development
   - production
   - utilization
   - support
   - retirement
4. 它强调可以 `iteratively and concurrently` 以及 `recursively` 应用。
5. 它还明确说：
   - 不规定特定 life cycle model
   - 不规定特定 development methodology / modelling approach / technique
   - information items 细节由 `ISO/IEC/IEEE 15289` 处理

## 核心内容摘录

### 生命周期框架定位

- Abstract 写道：该标准 `establishes a common framework of process descriptions for describing the life cycle of systems created by humans`

### 适用对象

- 适用于：
  - `systems of interest`
  - `their system elements`
  - `systems of systems`

### 生命周期覆盖范围

- 适用于：
  - `conception`
  - `development`
  - `production`
  - `utilization`
  - `support`
  - `retirement`

### 方法中立

- 官方明确说：
  - `does not prescribe a specific system life cycle model`
  - `does not detail information items in terms of name, format, explicit content, and recording media`
