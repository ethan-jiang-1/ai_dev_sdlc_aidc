## Why This Matters

Topic 02 is partly about why managers and senior engineers become bottlenecks. Trust is one of the core reasons. This source links trust directly to adoption and productivity.

## Key Facts Captured

- DORA reports that developers who trust gen AI more see stronger productivity benefits.
- At the same time, trust is low enough that 39% of developers outside Google trust gen AI output only “a little” or “not at all.”
- DORA recommends five strategies to improve trust, beginning with clear acceptable-use policy.
- The article explicitly links organizational transparency about acceptable use with higher trust.

## Research Use

- Supports the seed claim that the human bottleneck is often trust calibration rather than raw approval volume alone.
- Gives Topic 02 a measurable way to discuss why “middle-loop” work exists: someone must calibrate when to trust, verify, reject, or escalate AI output.

## Caveats

- This source is about trust and productivity, not job design directly.
- It should be paired with sources on role evolution and organizational programs.
