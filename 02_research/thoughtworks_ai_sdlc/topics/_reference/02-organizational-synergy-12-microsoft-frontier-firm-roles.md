## Why This Matters

This source gives Topic 02 a macro labor-market and organizational-design signal. It moves the discussion from speculation about `agent bosses` into a documented enterprise trend about new AI-focused responsibilities and specialist roles.

## Key Facts Captured

- Microsoft frames 2025 as the year firms moved from experimenting with AI to rebuilding organizational work around it.
- The report defines `Frontier Firm` employees partly by organization-wide AI deployment, active use of agents, planned integration of agents, and a belief that agents are key to ROI.
- The report lists new roles organizations are considering over the next 12 to 18 months, including AI Trainer, AI Data Specialist, AI Security Specialist, AI Agent Specialist, AI ROI Analyst, and Chief AI Officer.
- The methodology is based on a large international survey of 31,000 knowledge workers across 31 markets plus qualitative research with customers and AI-native startups.

## Research Use

- Supports the claim that human-agent team management is becoming a formal organizational capability area, not just a futurist label.
- Gives Topic 02 stronger evidence that the `middle loop` can resolve into multiple formal roles rather than one title.
- Useful for narrowing the staff-role-redesign question: some coordination work is clearly being split into specialist functions instead of remaining implicit.

## Caveats

- This is macro workplace research, not a software-engineering-only study.
- It is stronger on role emergence than on the exact day-to-day tools or workflows for those roles.
