## Why This Matters

Topic 02 is partly about the bottleneck created when leadership remains outside the actual adoption loop. This source explains why executive sponsorship must be operational rather than ceremonial.

## Key Facts Captured

- GitHub argues AI transformation fails when leaders only approve budgets but do not actively shape the shift.
- The article describes an `Executive Enablement Loop` that continuously equips leaders with success stories, demos, and communications material.
- It also recommends creating a manager resource guide for discussing AI proficiency in 1:1s and team meetings.
- The overall model is a cascading communication and translation structure rather than a single memo.

## Research Use

- Supports the seed claim that management roles are evolving toward alignment, translation, and friction removal.
- Gives Topic 02 a concrete blueprint for reducing coordination drift between strategy, directors, managers, and teams.

## Caveats

- This is a practical playbook, not a neutral research paper.
- It is strongest as an implementation pattern rather than a theory source.
