## Why This Matters

This is one of the best current sources for explaining why different people interact with AI differently. It gives Topic 02 a richer model than “some engineers like AI and some don’t.”

## Key Facts Captured

- DORA says a builder’s trust in AI is often inversely proportional to their technical proficiency relative to the task.
- High delegated trust appears in certain mindsets and creates higher exposure to security risks and missed best practices.
- High-proficiency builders more often practice low, verified trust and demand transparency and control.
- DORA also argues organizations need fluid, multi-layered abstractions so people can choose different collaboration modes with AI.

## Research Use

- Supports the seed claim that middle-loop and staff-level work will revolve around trust calibration and abstraction-layer management.
- Explains why one-size-fits-all AI workflows drift across teams and require governance plus tooling variation.

## Caveats

- This is a conceptual model, not a job taxonomy.
- It is highly useful for design and governance, but less useful for quantifying organization chart changes by itself.
