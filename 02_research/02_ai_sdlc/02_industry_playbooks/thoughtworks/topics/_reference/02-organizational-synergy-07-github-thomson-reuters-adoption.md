## Why This Matters

This is a valuable enterprise adoption case because it turns abstract coordination advice into a concrete rollout model.

## Key Facts Captured

- Thomson Reuters used a structured pilot before scaling broadly.
- Their four-stream rollout included onboarding, training, measurement/reporting, and continuous-improvement practices.
- They built a community of champions and coupled it with structured training resources.
- Their expanded metrics included efficiency, code quality, satisfaction, and PR duration.
- GitHub’s writeup explicitly says AI tools complement human effort and should be kept collaborative.

## Research Use

- Supports the claim that successful adoption requires new organizational routines, not just licenses.
- Gives Topic 02 concrete evidence for champion networks, feedback loops, and adoption telemetry as part of the new control plane.

## Caveats

- This is a vendor-mediated case study.
- It reports a successful pattern, so it may underrepresent failure cases or adoption friction.
