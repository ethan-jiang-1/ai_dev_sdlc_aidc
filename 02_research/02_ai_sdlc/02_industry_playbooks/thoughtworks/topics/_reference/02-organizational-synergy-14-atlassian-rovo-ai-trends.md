## Why This Matters

This source provides a concrete observability surface for the `middle loop`. It shows that organizational AI adoption can be monitored through operational analytics such as active users, interactions, active agents, agent runs, and usage by app.

## Key Facts Captured

- Atlassian exposes organization-level Rovo trends under Adoption and Agents tabs.
- Adoption analytics include users with AI enabled, active users, total Rovo interactions, usage over time, and interactions by app.
- Agent analytics include active agents, agent runs, agents created, and agent usage over time by app.
- The trends can be exported as CSV and are positioned as the right place to understand how people are using AI across apps and where agents are most active.

## Research Use

- Strong support for the claim that middle-loop work needs an analytics surface, not just policies and training.
- Helps define concrete operator metrics for AI workforce management: active users, interactions, active agents, runs, and app-level distribution.
- Useful bridge between Topic 02 organizational enablement and Topic 03 or Topic 04 observability and governance concerns.

## Caveats

- These are vendor analytics surfaces and not a complete enterprise operating model.
- The docs show what can be measured, but not how organizations should react when the metrics point to drift or misuse.
