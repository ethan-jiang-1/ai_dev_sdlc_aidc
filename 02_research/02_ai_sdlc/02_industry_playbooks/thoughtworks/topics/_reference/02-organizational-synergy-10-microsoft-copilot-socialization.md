## Why This Matters

The seed discusses junior developers, identity, and learning. This report is valuable because it connects AI use not only to productivity, but to socialization and workplace integration.

## Key Facts Captured

- The report found an association between more frequent Copilot use and stronger workplace integration for new employees.
- Participants said Copilot helped them get unstuck, save time, stimulate creativity, and act as a nonjudgmental assistant.
- Interns learned to use Copilot through trial and error, peer tips, and training resources.
- This suggests AI changes not just execution speed, but how people acclimate, learn, and participate in teams.

## Research Use

- Supports the seed claim that AI changes the development path for juniors and reshapes how onboarding and skill acquisition happen.
- Helps Topic 02 move beyond abstract role design into the social dimension of human-agent collaboration.

## Caveats

- Study population was Microsoft interns, not senior engineers or cross-industry teams.
- It is strongest for onboarding and socialization, less direct for staff-level coordination work.
