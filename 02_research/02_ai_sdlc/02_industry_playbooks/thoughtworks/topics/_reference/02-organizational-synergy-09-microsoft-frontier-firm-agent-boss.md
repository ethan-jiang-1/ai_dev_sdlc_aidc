## Why This Matters

This is one of the most direct current sources for the seed hypothesis that new jobs will emerge around managing hybrid human-agent teams.

## Key Facts Captured

- Microsoft says every employee will increasingly become an `agent boss`.
- The report states that 28% of managers are considering hiring AI workforce managers to lead hybrid teams of people and agents.
- It also says 32% plan to hire AI agent specialists to design, develop, and optimize agents in the next 12 to 18 months.
- Leaders expect more work around redesigning business processes with AI, building multi-agent systems, training agents, and managing them.
- Microsoft also emphasizes organization-wide AI literacy and ongoing learning as the shift spreads.

## Research Use

- Strong support for the idea that middle-loop work is not a niche but an emerging class of management and specialist labor.
- Gives Topic 02 a concrete macro signal that org charts will adapt around agent coordination.

## Caveats

- This is a broad workplace report, not software-engineering-only research.
- The hiring figures reflect plans and expectations, not already-established new roles at scale.
