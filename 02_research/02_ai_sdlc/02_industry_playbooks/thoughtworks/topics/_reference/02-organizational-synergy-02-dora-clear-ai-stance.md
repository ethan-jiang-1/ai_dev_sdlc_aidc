## Why This Matters

This source is directly about organizational coordination. It explains why ambiguity around allowed AI usage creates friction, anxiety, and unsafe improvisation.

## Key Facts Captured

- DORA says ambiguity creates two harmful extremes: over-caution and over-permissive unvetted use.
- A clear and communicated AI stance amplifies the positive impact of AI on effectiveness, organizational performance, and throughput.
- DORA emphasizes that the key measure is not whether a policy document exists, but whether teams actually understand it.
- Example survey questions target clarity, support for experimentation, expectation of use, and whether policy applies to real work.

## Research Use

- Supports the seed claim that “governance waiting” is a real coordination bottleneck.
- Helps define one concrete responsibility of the middle loop: translating abstract policy into practical day-to-day operating guidance.

## Caveats

- This is a capability page, not a dedicated ethnographic study of team behavior.
- It identifies a high-value coordination capability but does not define a full operating model by itself.
