## Key Facts Captured

- MITRE says ATLAS is a globally accessible, living knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques for AI systems.
- It is based on real-world attack observations and realistic demonstrations from AI red teams and security groups.
- MITRE says ATLAS can inform security analysts and AI developers, enable internal red teaming, and help understand mitigation pathways.
- The fact sheet also notes that ATLAS has expanded to include more LLM attacks and case studies.
