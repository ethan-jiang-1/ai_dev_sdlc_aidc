## Why This Matters

This source connects code-generation acceleration with downstream quality gates. It is valuable because it treats testing, review, validation, and deployment readiness as the real bottlenecks.

## Key Facts Captured

- Salesforce reports a 30% increase in code entering production after AI tooling scaled.
- The article states the bottleneck moved from code creation to `preparing that code for safe shipment`.
- The response was not “trust the AI more,” but broader automation for validation, testing coverage, and review support.
- Salesforce emphasizes that generative or reasoning AI is not allowed to change production systems autonomously; human operators remain responsible for production-impacting decisions.

## Research Use

- Supports the Topic 01 claim that quality defense migrates toward tests, validation workflows, and deployment discipline.
- Provides concrete practice evidence that release safety and quality gates must scale with AI output.
- Supplies a direct bridge between engineering discipline and Topic 04’s trust / safety layer.

## Caveats

- This is a mature large-enterprise setting with substantial internal platform capability.
- It demonstrates one viable response pattern, not a universal baseline for smaller teams.
